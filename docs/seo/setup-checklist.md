# SEO Setup Checklist

These steps need your own accounts, so you do them yourself. Do them once the sitemap is live.

## Google Search Console

1. Go to [Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) and add `voxastudios.com` as a Domain property.
2. Follow Google's steps to verify the domain.
3. Under **Sitemaps**, submit `https://voxastudios.com/sitemap.xml`.
4. Search Google for `site:voxastudios.com`. If your site doesn't appear, use **URL Inspection** on the homepage and request indexing.
5. Search Google for `Voxa Studios Tampa` and note whether your site appears.
